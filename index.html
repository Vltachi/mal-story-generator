// how it works

THREE STEPS,
ZERO EFFORT

01 🔑
Connect your MAL account
Login once with your MyAnimeList account. Your profile photo, ratings, and library are loaded automatically.
02 ✅
Mark as Completed
Finish an anime or manga on MAL as usual. A banner appears instantly offering to generate your story.
03 📸
Download & Share
Your story is generated at 1080×1920px, ready to post on Instagram Stories. No editing required.
// features

EVERYTHING
AUTOMATIC

🎨
Dynamic background
The cover art becomes a blurred, color-rich background unique to each anime or manga.
⭐
Metal rating palette
Score colors range from dark bordeaux at 1 all the way to brilliant gold at 10 — distinct at every step.
👤
Your profile, auto-loaded
Your MAL profile photo appears on the card automatically. No manual uploads needed.
🖼️
Pixel-perfect export
Exported at exactly 1080×1920px using Canvas API — what you see in preview is what you get.
⚡
Instant detection
A banner appears the moment you mark something as Completed. No need to open the extension manually.
🔒
Private & secure
OAuth PKCE authentication. Your token stays on your machine. No data is sent to any external server.

INSTALL IN 30 SECONDS

Load the extension in developer mode — no Chrome Web Store needed.

# 1. Download and unzip the extension
↓ Download mal-story-extension.zip below
# 2. Open Chrome / Brave / Edge
→ Go to chrome://extensions
→ Enable Developer Mode
→ Click Load unpacked → select the folder
# 3. Done — visit any MAL anime page!
